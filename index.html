About easy recipes

Easy recipes are a great start for beginners. These recipes include cupcakes, various types of cookies, banana bread, and much more. If you are looking to start baking as a hobby or job for the first time these recipes and websites will suit you just right.

About moderate recipes

Moderate recipes are perfect for those who have just advanced from easy recipes and want to pursue a baking business or make cool things for your family and friends. Some examples include cinnamon rolls, layered cakes, and madeline cakes.

About hard recipes

Hard recipes are recommended for advanced bakers who have overcome the moderate recipes by absolutely nailing them. They are perfect if you want to open a baking business or bake exquisite things at home for yourself or your family and friends.
