Ingredients

Ingredients.

  1. 3 tbsp (45 mL) unsalted butter (plus extra for greasing)
  2. ¼ cup (60 mL) all-purpose flour
  3. ½ tsp (2 mL) salt
  4. Pinch of black pepper and freshly grated nutmeg
  5. 1 cup (250 mL) whole milk (warm)
  6. 1 ½ cups (375 mL) shredded Canadian Cheddar, Gruyère, or Swiss cheese
  7. 4 large eggs, separated (yolks and whites in clean bowls)
