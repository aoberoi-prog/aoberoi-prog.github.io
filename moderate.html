About moderate recipes

Moderate recipes are perfect for those who have just advanced from easy recipes and want to pursue a baking business or make cool things for your family and friends. Some examples include cinnamon rolls, layered cakes, and madeline cakes.

How to bake Cinnamon rolls

Ingredients.

  1. Whole milk: ¾ cup (180ml), warmed to 105°F–115°F (40°C–46°C)
  2. Instant or active dry yeast: 2 ¼ teaspoons (1 standard packet / 7g)
  3. Granulated sugar: ¼ cup (50g)
  4. Egg: 1 large, at room temperature
  5. Egg yolk: 1 large, at room temperature (adds extra richness and softness)
  6. Unsalted butter: ¼ cup (57g), melted and slightly cooled
  7. Bread flour (or all-purpose flour): 3 cups (360g), plus extra for dusting
  8. Salt: ¾ teaspoon
  9. Cream cheese
