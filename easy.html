About easy recipes

Easy recipes are a great start for beginners. These recipes include cupcakes, various types of cookies, banana bread, and much more. If you are looking to start baking as a hobby or job for the first time these recipes and websites will suit you just right.

Easy cookie recipe to bake

Salted Butter. Unsalted butter works well, you do not have to add more salt. No dairy? Make my dairy-free chocolate chip cookies or vegan chocolate chip cookies. Want to brown the butter? Make my brown butter chocolate chip cookies. Eggs. If you’d like to find great egg substitutions that still make perfect cookies – see my eggless chocolate chip cookies recipe. Granulated Sugar. You can use white sugar or organic cane sugar. Or, make brown sugar chocolate chip cookies and only use brown sugar. Light brown sugar. Dark brown sugar also works really well, it just imparts a slightly bolder molasses taste. All-purpose flour. I recommend using an unbleached, all-purpose flour to make these chocolate chip cookies. To make gluten-free cookies, use all-purpose gluten free baking flour. Or make my gluten-free chocolate chip cookies. Fine sea salt. I exclusively bake and cook with pure, fine sea salt. Sea salt is different than table salt (that is iodized). If using iodized table salt, decrease the amount to ½ teaspoon. Chocolate chips. As you can see from the photos, sometimes I use chocolate chunks, chopped chocolate, or chocolate chips. It doesn’t matter what you use, as long as you use 2 cups.
